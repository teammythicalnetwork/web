About Mythical Studios

More than a name. It's a place to build.

Mythical Studios brings together people who enjoy technology, creativity and the challenge of turning an idea into something real.

Who we are

Small team. Big imagination.

We are an independent creative technology studio working across artificial intelligence, gaming, web development and digital experiences.

We don't want to simply talk about future technology. We want to experiment with it, build with it and learn what makes it genuinely useful.

Some projects start as a tiny idea. Others grow into full platforms. Our approach stays the same: understand the idea, build a first version, learn from it and keep improving.
